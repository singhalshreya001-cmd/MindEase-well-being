A NOTE ON GETTING SUPPORT

You deserve support beyond a screen.

If your feelings are persistent, difficult to manage, or affecting daily life, consider talking to someone you trust or a qualified mental health professional.

Need urgent help in India?

If you are in immediate danger, call India's emergency number.

Call 112

For mental health support, you can also check the Government of India's Tele-MANAS service at telemanas.mohfw.gov.in . Check the official site for current contact details and availability.
